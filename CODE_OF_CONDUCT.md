# Code of Conduct

Contributors are expected to keep project discussions professional, technical, and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Give actionable feedback focused on code and product behavior.
- Respect different experience levels and viewpoints.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, or publishing private information.
- Do not post secrets, credentials, personal data, or exploit details in public issues.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that violates these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or users.

Security reports should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
