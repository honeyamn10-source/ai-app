# BYAK AI Privacy Policy — release template

**Status:** Template requiring the operator's legal name, address, contact, retention periods and production vendors before publication.

BYAK AI processes account identity, app settings, user-created chats/projects, uploaded content, provider-connection metadata, encrypted API credentials, subscription state, usage counts, security events and device/session information to operate and secure the service. The app should collect no unnecessary contacts, location, advertising identifier or background sensor data.

When a user selects an AI provider or external research source, relevant prompts or queries are sent to that third party under its own terms. BYAK AI does not create provider accounts and does not control provider-side retention.

Users can view, export and delete their content, memory, connections and account. Production retention windows, backups and legal exceptions must be stated explicitly. Deleted data should leave active systems promptly and expire from backups on the published schedule.

The operator must identify all subprocessors, cross-border transfers, safeguards, age restrictions, Canadian privacy obligations, Google Play Data Safety declarations and a privacy/security contact before release.
